Behind the Scenes

A Day in the Life of a FreshPetal Order

🌙
Previous Evening — by 8 PM
Orders Cut Off
All orders placed before 8 PM are confirmed for next-morning delivery. Our system groups them by cluster area and generates a picking list automatically.
🌃
3:30 AM
Team Heads to the Wholesale Market
Our sourcing team reaches Uppal Wholesale Flower Market and Begum Bazar by 3:30–4:00 AM — when the freshest stock arrives from farms in Coimbatore, Bangalore & local Ranga Reddy growers.
🌅
4:00 AM – 5:00 AM
Picking, Packing & Weighing
Each order is individually weighed (50g accuracy), packed in fresh moisture bags, and labeled with the customer's flat number. Pooja essentials (coconut, agarbatti, etc.) are added as a bundle.
🛵
5:00 AM – 7:00 AM
Cluster Delivery Route
Riders depart with sorted bags for each cluster. Each rider covers 8–12 societies on a tight loop — up to 4 deliveries per minute during the route. Flowers left at your door, letterbox, or with the society guard desk.
🌸
By 6:00–7:00 AM
Fresh Flowers at Your Door
You open your door to find your fragrant blooms waiting. Still dewy-fresh from the market. Your mandir is ready, your morning pooja can begin — no trip needed.

Quick Answers

Can I order individual flowers by weight?

Yes! Every flower is available from 50g. You set the quantity on the Shop page and price is shown live before adding to cart.

What happens if I miss the order cutoff?

Orders placed after 8 PM are scheduled for the day after tomorrow. You can also WhatsApp us for urgent same-day arrangements (subject to stock).

What if nobody is home for delivery?

We leave the flowers at your door or hand them to the security desk. You can also leave delivery instructions in your account settings.

Can I subscribe to a custom mix of flowers?

Absolutely! Use the Custom Plan Builder on the Subscriptions page to pick exactly which flowers and pooja items you want on your schedule.
