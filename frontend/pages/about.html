How It Started

A Flower Shop Idea That Grew

It started sitting in our family flower shop in Uppal. Every morning, the same apartment residents would come in — still in their house clothes — to buy marigold, banti, and a coconut for their daily mandir.

That's when the idea hit: what if we deliver to their door at 6 AM? No morning rush, no carrying flowers on a scooter, no running out of essentials on a festival day.

We started with one society in Uppal. Within 3 months, 8 apartment complexes in the Uppal–Shamshabad belt had joined. FreshPetal was born.

Our Model

The Cluster Delivery System

We don't do individual point-to-point delivery (which is expensive). Instead, we group nearby apartment complexes into delivery clusters.

One rider covers 8–12 societies in a tight geographic loop. This means:

💸 Lower delivery cost → FREE delivery on packages
⏱️ Consistent 5:30–7 AM slot for all societies
🌱 Less traffic, less fuel, more eco-friendly
